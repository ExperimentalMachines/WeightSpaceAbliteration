## Notes

- **Derivation/evaluation disjointness is not automatic in these repos.** The refusal_direction pipeline dedupes train against val/test (leaving 1 stray prompt), and Geometry dedupes SALAD against its test set, but Heretic derives and evaluates on two splits of the *same* AdvBench corpus, COSMIC documents HarmBench inside its derivation pool, and OBLITERATUS offers HarmBench as a derivation source while HarmBench is the standard test set. Any of those three turns the probe's "held-out category" claim into a within-corpus split.
- **AdvBench and HarmBench are clean at string level (0 shared prompts) but not at the level of provenance.** TDC2023 and JailbreakBench each carry substantial exact overlap with HarmBench, so a study that pools "all public harmful sets" for derivation will pull HarmBench rows in through the side door.
- **SALAD-bench is the quietest leakage path.** It is 21,318 prompts of which 359 are AdvBench and 4,184 are HH-harmless; using it as the harmful pole (Geometry of Refusal) puts 212 AdvBench strings into the derivation set, which matters only if AdvBench is later used for the probe or the eval.
- **Heretic ships no prompt files.** Its "bundled" sets are HF dataset references in TOML (`mlabonne/harmful_behaviors` train[:400], `mlabonne/harmless_alpaca` train[:400]); evaluation reuses the test splits of the same two datasets. The `mlabonne` cards carry no license field, so redistribution terms are UNVERIFIED even though the underlying AdvBench/Alpaca terms are MIT and CC-BY-NC-4.0 respectively.
- **`harmbench/behaviors` does not exist on the HF API** (404), yet OBLITERATUS's `_load_harmbench` calls it; the working mirror is `walledai/HarmBench` (MIT, gated auto). Reproduce the Obliteratus baseline with the mirror and note the substitution.
- **Beyond I'm Sorry has no locatable artifact.** The paper describes "open-sourced code and feature indices" and a filtered 861-prompt D_harmful, but no repo/dataset was found; treat its feature lists (2,538 Gemma-2-2B / 110 LLaMA-3.1-8B) as unavailable until a link appears.
- **Gated sources** (`sorry-bench/*`, `allenai/wildguardmix`, `allenai/wildjailbreak`, `walledai/*`) need an HF token and terms acceptance; row counts for the first three could not be API-verified and are recorded as UNVERIFIED rather than quoted from secondary sources.
- **Licenses that block redistribution:** Alpaca and the PKU sets are CC-BY-NC-4.0; SALAD-bench and the COSMIC/TruthfulQA vendored file are Apache-2.0; Geometry-of-Refusal has no LICENSE file, so its derived splits are all-rights-reserved by default; MaliciousInstruct and the `mlabonne` re-splits have no license file/field at all.
