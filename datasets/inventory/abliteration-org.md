# abliteration.org/datasets — EVALUATE + OPEN ONLY, top 60 by downloads

Audit date **2026-11-07**. Source page rendered at `https://abliteration.org/datasets/all` with the **EVALUATE** stage chip active and **OPEN ONLY** checked: 824 of 885 EVALUATE sets are open, and the list was kept on its default sort, *Most downloaded*. The top 60 distinct `owner/name` ids were followed to Hugging Face. Everything below rank 60 (764 open EVALUATE sets) is **not audited — stage+download rank below cutoff**; the 61 gated EVALUATE sets are excluded by the OPEN ONLY filter, and the other five stages (EXTRACT 1,521, ABLATE 327, HEAL 229, FINE-TUNE 362, MISC 754) were not in scope.

Of the 60, 39 are capability/instruction-following benchmarks, mirrors or SFT corpora, 11 are result/log artefacts, and only 10 carry behaviour-bearing prompt rows. Five showed any exact-normalised collision: `JailbreakBench/JBB-Behaviors` (6/50 AdvBench, 3/50 HarmBench), `swiss-ai/harmbench` (0/50, 50/50), `andyrdt/gpt-oss-20b-rollouts` (0/50, 36/50; an artefact), `allenai/tulu-3-harmbench-eval` (0/50, 50/50) and `AlignmentResearch/HarmBench` (0/50, 36/50) — the last three are HarmBench repackagings.

Revisions are the 40-hex `sha` returned by `GET https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/<id>` on 2026-11-07; all 60 resolved, all 60 report `gated: false` and carry a 40-hex revision. Row counts are summed over all splits in the dataset-viewer `/size` response unless a file-level count is given. Overlap is **exact-normalised** (NFKC, lowercase, non-alphanumerics collapsed to single spaces) between any string cell in the first 50 rows of the stated config/split and the 520 AdvBench `goal` strings / the 320 HarmBench text-test `Behavior` strings (the study's 313 after its collision exclusions). Substring counts and the matched strings are carried in the JSON; a 0 means no sampled string equalled either reference after normalisation.

## Worth adding

None of the six showed an exact-normalised collision with AdvBench or HarmBench in its 50-row sample. Semantic dedup against the 313-row HarmBench text-test endpoint is still to be run, so each figure is an upper bound.

1. **OpenSafetyLab/Salad-Data** — 21,318 base questions (17,134 harmful + 4,184 HH-harmless + other sources) plus 5,000 attack-enhanced paraphrases of base questions; 6 domains, 16 tasks, 65 categories. Sample 0/0 exact against AdvBench/HarmBench; the card declares 359 AdvBench-derived rows, leaving ≈16,775 unique harmful behaviours before semantic dedup. Apache-2.0. **Adds ≈17k harmful questions to the denominator** (the 5k attack rows are surface variants, not new behaviours) — roughly fifty times the current 313-row endpoint.
2. **IS2Lab/S-Eval** — English attack set 100,000 at full scale (nested 10k/30k/50k/100k; 10k base + 100k attack in each of English and Chinese). Sample 0/0 exact. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — noncommercial, flag it in the pre-registration. **Adds up to 100k English attack prompts**, or 10k at the cheapest scale.
3. **nvidia/Nemotron-Safety-Guard-Dataset-v3** — 514,617 rows across 12 languages. Sample 0/0 exact (first 50 rows were Arabic). CC BY 4.0. Synthetic CultureGuard adaptation/translation of Aegis 2.0 plus curated jailbreaks, so it **adds ~514k multilingual prompts (harmful and benign mixed) but not independently sourced behaviours**; neither the English nor the harmful share is published.
4. **walledai/WildGuardTest** — 1,725 prompt-harm/response-harm/refusal-labelled items. Sample 0/0 exact. ODC-BY. **Adds 1,725 harm/unharmful-labelled probes** for judge calibration and the over-refusal arm; `sbintuitions/WildGuardTestJP` (1,725, 0/0) is a translation, not a separate gain.
5. **bench-llm/or-bench** — 1,319 hard safe + 655 toxic prompts (plus 80,359 easy safe). Sample 0/0 exact on hard-1k. CC BY 4.0. **Adds a 1,974-prompt over-refusal/null arm**, not harmful-denominator rows; the three OR-Bench mirrors carry the same 82,333 rows and must not be counted three times.
6. **Roblox/RobloxGuard-Eval** — 2,873 expert-labelled prompt/response pairs over 25 policy subcategories. Sample 0/0 exact. CC BY-NC 4.0. **Adds a 2,873-row held-out domain** with response-level violation labels, independent of the OpenAI-policy taxonomies behind HarmBench/JBB/SALAD.

`JailbreakBench/JBB-Behaviors` is **not** a new addition: already in the study inventory as HELDOUT. Measured collision in its 100-row harmful split is 6/50 AdvBench and 3/50 HarmBench exact in the first 50 rows (full split: 11/100 AdvBench, 7/100 HarmBench; 18 rows are declared AdvBench-sourced).
